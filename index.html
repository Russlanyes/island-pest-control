Meet Darío Reyes

Founder · Agricultural engineer (UNAG)

Island Pest Control & Garden Care is led by Darío Reyes, an agricultural engineer trained at UNAG, Honduras' National University of Agriculture.

That background is the difference: integrated pest management and garden care built on knowing how insects, plants and soil work. The goal is safe, efficient methods and long-lasting results, for your home, your business and your garden.
